Help

Write a note

Start typing in the writing area above your notes. Your writing is saved as you go.

Find a note

Use the search icon to search titles and content, or open Dates and options to jump to a day.

Edit a note

Tap or click a note to edit it. Choose Done to close the editor.

Note actions on a phone

Use the quick actions beside a note or tap the three-dot button for the rest. Swipe left to open the menu and right to close it.

Change the view

Open Controls to choose quick actions, density, hidden notes, and date sections. You can change note font and size there too.

Welcome to gnotes

  1. Write your first noteType a title or some text in the writing area. Your draft saves as you type. Tap Save on a phone, or click outside the writing area on a larger screen.
  2. Find it againSaved notes appear below the writing area. Use Search in the header to find a note later.
